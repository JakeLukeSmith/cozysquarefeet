Privacy policy

Last updated 04 October 2026

Cozy Square Feet is a small Pinterest business account and website sharing ideas for small apartments, plus a free printable guide. This policy covers this website, our email list and Cozy Square Feet Scheduler, the internal tool we use to publish to our own Pinterest account.

Cozy Square Feet Scheduler and Pinterest

Cozy Square Feet Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of the Cozy Square Feet Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

When we connect it to Pinterest, we authorize it with our own account through Pinterest's official sign-in (OAuth). It uses the Pinterest API only to:

  • create and list boards on our own account;
  • publish our own original Pins (our images, titles, descriptions and links to this website);
  • read the analytics of our own Pins and account (impressions, saves, outbound clicks) so we can decide what to make next.

It does not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users. It does not follow, comment, message or save other people's content, and it publishes at a normal pace (around 8 Pins a day). The access token and the Pin statistics it reads are stored privately on our own computer, are never sold or shared, and are deleted when no longer needed. We can revoke the tool's access at any time in Pinterest under Settings → Security → Apps. Our use of the Pinterest API follows Pinterest's Developer Guidelines.

This website

This website uses no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Email list

If you sign up for the free guide, we collect only the email address you enter, to send the guide and occasional tips. Our email provider stores it on our behalf. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we never sell or share your address.
